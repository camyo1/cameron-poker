Quick tip

A pair is two cards of the same rank. A flush is five cards of the same suit. A straight is five consecutive ranks. Your best hand uses any combination of your two cards and the five community cards.

How Texas Hold'em works

  1. Each player receives two private cards.
  2. The flop reveals three shared community cards.
  3. The turn reveals a fourth community card.
  4. The river reveals the fifth community card.
  5. If both players remain, the best five-card hand wins the pot.

Actions

Check: Pass without betting when no bet is owed.
Call: Match the computer's current bet.
Raise: Increase the bet to put more chips in the pot.
Fold: Give up the hand and let the opponent take the pot.
